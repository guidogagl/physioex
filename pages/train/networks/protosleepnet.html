ProtoSleepNet and ProtoSleepTransformer#

Two pretrained sleep stagers, trained on 28 sites from 12 public and clinical datasets and published on the Hugging Face Hub in 4rooms/physioex:

Both are physioex.models.protosleepnet.ProtoSleepNet networks: a per-channel epoch encoder, a channel mixer that learns how much to trust each modality, a residual sequence encoder and a linear classifier. They are the models of the paper Prototype-based interpretable sleep staging with physiologically meaningful sub-stage pattern discovery (npj Digital Medicine; code and analyses in guidogagl/protosleepnet), retrained on many sites at once. The weights of the paper itself (one dataset per model) are a separate archive.

Quick start#

import torch
from physioex.models import load_from_pretrained

model = load_from_pretrained("protosleeptransformer-gagliardi", verbose=True)   # eval mode, CPU

# (batch, L, channels, T, F): log-power STFT spectrograms of EEG, EOG, EMG (T=29, F=129)
x = torch.randn(2, 21, 3, 29, 129)
with torch.no_grad():
    logits = model(x)            # (2, 21, 5): W, N1, N2, N3, REM

verbose=True prints the per-site test metrics stored with the weights. The input is what the seqsleepnet pipeline preset produces for the channels ["EEG", "EOG", "EMG"], so the models can be fed directly from any physioex dataset:

from physioex.data import get_dataset

ds = get_dataset("hmc")(channels=["EEG", "EOG", "EMG"], pipelines="seqsleepnet", sequence_length=21)

encode(x) returns the contextualised epoch embeddings (B, L, d_model). forward(x, quantize=True) replaces the epoch embeddings by their nearest prototype, but it needs a codebook (set_codebook) that is learned after training from the epoch embeddings; the checkpoints on the Hub contain no codebook, and quantize=False (the default) is the plain classifier. The prototype analyses of the paper are in its repository, and relevance maps for these models are in Explainability (LRP supports the default, non-quantised forward).

Recipe (the same for both models): 3 channels (EEG, EOG, EMG), seqsleepnet pipeline, site-balanced sampling (make_site_sampler, every site gets the same probability mass, see Data), batch size 32, learning rate 1e-4 with a reduce-on-plateau scheduler, no weight decay, 300,000 windows per epoch, at most 20 epochs, channel dropout 0.5 in the channel mixer, seed 42. The released checkpoint is the one with the highest macro-averaged validation kappa among the evaluated checkpoints (up to 30 validation subjects per site): step 161,500 (epoch 17) for PSN and step 129,000 (epoch 13) for PST. Neighbouring checkpoints differ by about 0.001 kappa, so the choice is not critical.

Performance by training site#

Metrics are computed on the test subjects of each site, pooling the epochs of all of them (this is not a mean over nights), with full-night sliding-window voting (windows of L epochs at L different offsets, overlapping predictions averaged). Accuracy, Cohen’s kappa and macro F1 over the five AASM stages. Read the tables with these limits in mind:

  • The sites were seen in training (other subjects of the same sites), so this is not external validation: a new site, montage or population can perform differently.

  • Single training run (one seed), no confidence intervals. Several sites have 2-6 test subjects, so their values are noisy; SHHS is by far the largest test set (842 nights).

  • Per-site values mix population, scoring protocol and recording hardware; do not read a difference between two sites as a difference in model quality without checking the labels.

The two single-centre studies at the end of the training table (alzheimers_hc, parkinsons_night_hoa) were part of training but their per-site metrics are not published, so the tables below list 26 of the 28 sites.

Intended use and limits#

For research on sleep staging and its interpretability; not a medical device, and not for clinical diagnosis. The models expect the spectrogram input described above: a different preprocessing, signal amplitude scale or montage changes the input distribution, so check the staging on a sample of your own data before relying on it. The channel set is fixed to EEG, EOG and EMG (the channel mixer was trained with random channel dropout, which makes the models tolerant to a missing channel, but this was not evaluated per site here).
